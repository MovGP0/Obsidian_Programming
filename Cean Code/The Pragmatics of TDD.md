**The Pragmatics of TDD** summarizes clean-code-style guidance from [The Pragmatics of TDD](https://blog.cleancoder.com/uncle-bob/2013/03/06/ThePragmaticsOfTDD.html).

TDD is presented as a practical discipline for controlling defects, design pressure, and developer
confidence. Its value depends on keeping the loop small and refactoring seriously.

## Style Guidance

- Treat tests as executable examples of intended behavior.
- Use failures to guide the next small implementation step.
- Do not skip refactoring; otherwise TDD degenerates into merely accumulating regression tests.
