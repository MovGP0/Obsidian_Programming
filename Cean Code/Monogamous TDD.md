**Monogamous TDD** summarizes clean-code-style guidance from [Monogamous TDD](https://blog.cleancoder.com/uncle-bob/2014/04/25/MonogamousTDD.html).

TDD works best when attention stays on one failing test and one small behavior at a time. Jumping among many simultaneous failures weakens the feedback loop.

## Style Guidance

- Add one failing test, make it pass, then refactor.
- Keep the red-green-refactor cycle small enough to preserve confidence.
- Use the discipline to control scope during design exploration.
