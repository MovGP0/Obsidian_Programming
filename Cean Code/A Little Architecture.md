**A Little Architecture** summarizes clean-code-style guidance from [A Little Architecture](https://blog.cleancoder.com/uncle-bob/2016/01/04/ALittleArchitecture.html).

Architecture is about the important decisions that keep options open. Even small systems need enough structure to protect policy from premature commitment to details.

## Style Guidance

- Defer hard-to-reverse detail decisions when the core policy can be designed without them.
- Use boundaries proportionate to the risk and lifespan of the system.
- Architecture should serve changeability, not produce diagrams detached from code.
