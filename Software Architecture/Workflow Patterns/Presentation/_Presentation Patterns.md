title: Presentation Patterns
---
Presentation patterns describe how workflow patterns are made visible without confusing the meaning of a workflow with the notation used to draw or write it. They separate two questions that are often blended together in modeling work: what semantic structure is being represented, and how that structure is rendered for readers, tools, and reviewers.

This separation matters because workflow patterns are meant to compare languages and systems. A useful comparison cannot depend only on whether two diagrams look alike. It has to ask whether the same underlying control-flow, data, resource, or exception-handling concept can be represented, and then whether the chosen notation makes that concept clear enough to inspect and communicate.
