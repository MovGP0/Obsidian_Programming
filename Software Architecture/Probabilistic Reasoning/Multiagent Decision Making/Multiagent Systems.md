---
title: Multiagent Systems
---
**Multiagent systems** contain multiple autonomous agents that interact in a shared environment.

Agents may cooperate, compete, communicate, negotiate, or simply interfere with each other.

## Design questions

| Question | Why it matters |
| -------- | -------------- |
| Are agents cooperative or competitive? | Changes the objective and protocol. |
| Is communication reliable? | Affects coordination. |
| Are agents rational, learned, or rule-based? | Affects modelling assumptions. |
| Is the environment fully observable? | Determines whether belief tracking is needed. |

## Common concerns

- coordination,
- negotiation,
- opponent modelling,
- mechanism design,
- distributed planning,
- emergent behavior.

## Use when

- decisions are distributed across actors,
- central planning is unavailable or undesirable,
- each actor has its own information or incentives.
