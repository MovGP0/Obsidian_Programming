---
title: Expected Utility
---
**Expected utility** combines probabilities with preferences.

For an action $a$:

$$
EU(a) = \sum_s P(s \mid a) U(s)
$$

| Symbol | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| $s$ | Possible outcome state. |
| $P(s \mid a)$ | Probability of outcome $s$ after action $a$. |
| $U(s)$ | Utility of outcome $s$. |

The rational action is the one with maximum expected utility, assuming the probabilities and utilities are correct.

## Expected value vs. expected utility

Expected value uses a numeric outcome such as money. Expected utility uses a preference function. Utility can encode risk aversion, risk seeking, safety constraints, or nonlinear preferences.

## Use when

- actions have uncertain outcomes,
- outcomes have different preferences or costs,
- the best decision is not the most likely outcome,
- risk sensitivity matters.
