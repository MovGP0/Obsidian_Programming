OkCupid analyzes technical properties of profile photos, such as flash, depth of field, time of day, and camera type. The study combines 552,000 example photos with 11.4 million user opinions.

## Key findings

- Photo technique changed how other users rated a picture.
- Flash was associated with an older apparent age.
- Shallow depth of field and some shooting times were associated with better ratings.

## Algorithm relevance

This is a ranking study based on observed reactions. It is separate from the question-based match percentage, but it shows how OkCupid used interaction data to optimize presentation.
