OkCupid analyzes first contacts and replies from more than one million users to measure race-related messaging patterns.

## Key findings

- Reply rates differed by sender and recipient race.
- Stated acceptance of interracial relationships did not remove behavioral differences.
- The aggregate tables describe platform behavior and not individual intent.

## Algorithm relevance

Observed interaction data contains social bias. A recommender that learns from replies can reproduce this bias even when the explicit compatibility questions are neutral.
