OkCupid critiques paid dating sites with public figures, activity estimates, and business-model arguments. The author also states his conflict of interest as an OkCupid founder.

## Key findings

- Inactive profiles can make a catalog look larger than its reachable population.
- A subscription business can benefit when matching takes longer.
- Several conclusions depend on assumptions about competitor data and activity.

## Algorithm relevance

A matching system must measure reachable, active candidates. Catalog size alone is not an adequate coverage metric.
