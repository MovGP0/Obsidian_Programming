This micro-post maps answers to one sexual-behavior question by age and location. An interactive slider showed how the geographic distribution changed from age 18 to 40.

## Key findings

- One match question supported geographic and age-based analysis.
- The post highlights Pittsburgh as an unusual local result.
- The author offers possible explanations but does not test them.

## Algorithm relevance

A question can have high descriptive value without having high compatibility value. Geographic variation alone does not prove that the item should rank early.
