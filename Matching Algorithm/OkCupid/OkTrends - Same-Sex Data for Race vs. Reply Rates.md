This addendum presents race and reply-rate tables for gay and lesbian users. It compares them with the earlier heterosexual analysis.

## Key findings

- Race-related reply differences were present in the same-sex data.
- Several subgroups had small samples and noisy estimates.
- Patterns differed by gender and orientation.

## Algorithm relevance

The post shows why aggregate compatibility or response models need uncertainty estimates and minimum sample thresholds.
