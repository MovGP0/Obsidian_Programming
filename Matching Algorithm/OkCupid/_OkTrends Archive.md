## Archive method

The scraper queried the Internet Archive CDX index for HTTP, HTTPS, www, and non-www variants of the OkTrends host. It found 411 unique successful HTML URLs. It removed administration pages, feeds, assets, yearly indexes, comment pages, and duplicate dated or undated article URLs. This left 31 distinct articles and micro-posts.

Each note records the original URL, a normalized Wayback link, and the capture timestamp used for extraction. The archived HTML was converted to plain text in a temporary working folder. The final vault contains paraphrased notes and a metadata manifest, not verbatim copies of the full posts.

## Interpretation limits

- OkTrends usually analyzed a self-selected population of OkCupid users. It did not sample the general population.
- Most results are observational correlations. They do not establish causes.
- Historical categories and language can be sensitive or outdated.
- Archive captures can omit images, scripts, or interactive controls.
- An OkTrends analysis can describe platform behavior without describing the production matching algorithm.
