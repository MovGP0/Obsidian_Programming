This addendum compares message-length and reply-rate curves for same-sex contacts with the earlier heterosexual charts.

## Key findings

- Women contacting women had a curve similar to men contacting women.
- The post presents separate curves rather than assuming one universal messaging model.
- Some groups had smaller samples and more uncertain estimates.

## Algorithm relevance

Segment-specific response models can be more accurate than one global model. The same principle applies to adaptive question selection.
