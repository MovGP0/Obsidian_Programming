OkCupid compares profile claims about height, income, and photo age with population distributions and observed behavior to estimate systematic misrepresentation.

## Key findings

- Reported height was shifted upward compared with reference data.
- Reported income became less plausible at older ages.
- Profile photos often became older while the account remained active.

## Algorithm relevance

Explicit input is not always reliable. A compatibility system can benefit from validation and confidence estimates, but hidden corrections also create trust and fairness risks.
