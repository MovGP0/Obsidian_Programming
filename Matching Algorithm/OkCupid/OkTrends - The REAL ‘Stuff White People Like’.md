OkCupid analyzes 280 million words from 526,000 profile essays and identifies phrases that distinguish self-reported racial groups.

## Key findings

- The method ranks phrases by how distinctive they are for a group.
- Common interests are not necessarily discriminating features.
- The results reflect language used on OkCupid and can reproduce stereotypes if applied carelessly.

## Algorithm relevance

The post demonstrates feature selection by relative distinctiveness. It also shows the fairness risk of using demographic proxies in recommendation models.
