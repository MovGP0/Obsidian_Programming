This micro-post compares the length of women's replies by age. The displayed trend rises with age and levels off in the late twenties.

## Key findings

- Reply length varied with age in the observed sample.
- The post does not identify the cause of the pattern.
- It is an outtake from the older-women analysis.

## Algorithm relevance

The post is a reminder that engagement metrics vary across groups. A model can confuse group composition with individual compatibility if it does not control for this variation.
