OkCupid analyzes age preferences, attractiveness, message volume, reply rates, and relationship-status data to argue that men overlook compatible older women.

## Key findings

- Men's stated age ranges and message behavior favored younger women.
- High message volume reduced the reply chance from heavily contacted users.
- Age changed both demand and response patterns.

## Algorithm relevance

The post shows the difference between compatibility and market competition. A strong match score does not ensure contact or a reply when attention is unevenly distributed.
