OkCupid maps answers to selected match questions across US states and compares each state with the national average.

## Key findings

- Question answers showed regional patterns.
- The maps use relative rates, not raw population totals.
- The post combines serious and playful examples.

## Algorithm relevance

Regional answer distributions can change the apparent information value of a question. A selector can use global statistics or adjust them for a local population.
