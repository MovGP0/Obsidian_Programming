OkCupid revisits racial attraction and messaging patterns with five more years of interaction data. It compares the newer results with the 2009 analysis.

## Key findings

- Many group-level attraction patterns remained visible over time.
- Stated views about interracial relationships did not fully match observed behavior.
- The results concern aggregate behavior and must not be used as rules about individuals.

## Algorithm relevance

The post separates explicit questionnaire answers from revealed interaction behavior. A hybrid recommender can use both, but it must also address bias and fairness.
