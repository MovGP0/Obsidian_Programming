OkCupid compares average attractiveness ratings with disagreement among raters and the number of messages a woman receives.

## Key findings

- Women with the same average rating could receive different message volumes.
- Greater disagreement was associated with more messages in some rating ranges.
- A polarizing photo could outperform a broadly acceptable photo.

## Algorithm relevance

Averages can hide useful variance. Ranking models can need the full response distribution, not only one mean score.
