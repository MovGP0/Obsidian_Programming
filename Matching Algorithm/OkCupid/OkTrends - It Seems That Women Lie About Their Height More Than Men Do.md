This micro-post compares women's reported height with reported sexual orientation and then compares the height distribution with population data.

## Key findings

- The orientation correlation was weaker for women than for men.
- Very short and very tall women had higher reported gay or bisexual rates in the sample.
- The reported-height distribution suggested rounding or self-presentation effects.

## Algorithm relevance

Self-reported profile fields can contain systematic measurement error. Models should not treat them as exact physical measurements.
