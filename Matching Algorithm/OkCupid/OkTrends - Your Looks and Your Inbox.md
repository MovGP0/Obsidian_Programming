OkCupid relates photo attractiveness ratings to incoming messages and to replies on outgoing messages.

## Key findings

- Incoming attention was highly concentrated among the top-rated profiles.
- Attractiveness affected response rates as well as message volume.
- Men and women showed different contact distributions.

## Algorithm relevance

Compatibility, visibility, and attractiveness are separate ranking signals. A system that optimizes only for likely replies can reinforce existing attention inequality.
