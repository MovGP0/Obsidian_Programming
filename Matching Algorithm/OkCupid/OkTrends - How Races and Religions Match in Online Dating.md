OkCupid explains that users define their own matching rules through answers, accepted partner answers, and importance. It then compares compatibility patterns across race and religion.

## Key findings

- A match percentage summarizes how well two users satisfy each other's stated requirements.
- Race and religion were associated with different compatibility patterns.
- The post distinguishes compatibility scores from actual message behavior.

## Algorithm relevance

This is one of the strongest primary sources for the original [[OkCupid Matching Algorithm]]. It confirms that the score came from user-defined preferences rather than a fixed staff-written personality model.
