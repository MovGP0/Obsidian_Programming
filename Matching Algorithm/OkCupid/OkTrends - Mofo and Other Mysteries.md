OkCupid presents unusual distributions from user-submitted match questions. The post treats them as statistical outtakes instead of a single model of compatibility.

## Key findings

- The question catalog included many informal and culturally specific items.
- Large response counts made even odd questions suitable for population analysis.
- Question wording and context limit interpretation.

## Algorithm relevance

The examples show why a question selector needs coverage, discrimination, moderation, and redundancy controls in addition to raw popularity.
