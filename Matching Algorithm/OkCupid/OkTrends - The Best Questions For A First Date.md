OkCupid searches 275,294 match questions and 776 million answers for ordinary questions that predict answers to more sensitive relationship questions.

## Key findings

- The source gives an exact count of 275,294 match questions in February 2011.
- The analysis selects accessible proxy questions that correlate with deeper preferences.
- The beer question is presented as a predictor of willingness to have sex on a first date.

## Algorithm relevance

This is a direct example of feature selection and proxy questions. It supports the information-gain reconstruction in the [[OkCupid Question Selection Algorithm]].
