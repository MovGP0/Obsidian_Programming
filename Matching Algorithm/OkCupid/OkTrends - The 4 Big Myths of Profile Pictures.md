OkCupid codes more than 7,000 profile photos by facial expression, setting, and visible skin, then relates these features to messages and conversations.

## Key findings

- Several common rules about eye contact, smiling, and photo context did not hold for every group.
- A photo could attract messages but not longer conversations.
- Age and gender changed some associations.

## Algorithm relevance

The post separates an immediate ranking signal from a later outcome. Matching systems should choose a target that represents the desired result, not only clicks or first contacts.
