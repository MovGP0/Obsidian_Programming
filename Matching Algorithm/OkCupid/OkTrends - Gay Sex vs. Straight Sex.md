OkCupid compares stated sexuality, attraction, and relationship behavior across gay and straight users. It uses site data to challenge common claims about sexual orientation.

## Key findings

- The post separates identity, attraction, and behavior instead of treating them as one variable.
- It compares patterns across age and gender.
- The analysis is observational and uses a self-selected dating-site population.

## Algorithm relevance

The post shows why matching systems must keep distinct variables separate. A single demographic label cannot replace explicit preferences and accepted answers.
