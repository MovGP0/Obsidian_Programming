OkCupid analyzes more than 500,000 first messages and relates words, phrases, grammar, and greetings to reply rates.

## Key findings

- Informal spelling and generic compliments were associated with lower reply rates.
- Specific interests and some self-effacing language were associated with higher rates.
- The study reports correlations and cannot prove that a phrase causes a reply.

## Algorithm relevance

The post is an early feature-selection example. It ranks message features by predictive value, which is related to but separate from the question selector.
