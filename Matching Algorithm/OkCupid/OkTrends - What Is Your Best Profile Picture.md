OkCupid introduces MyBestFace, a tool that collected pairwise votes to help a person select their best profile photo. It produced a personal ranking instead of one global beauty score.

## Key findings

- The tool compared photos of the same person.
- Votes could be filtered to a relevant audience.
- More comparisons increased confidence in the ranking.

## Algorithm relevance

Pairwise comparison is an alternative data-collection method. It is useful when absolute ratings are noisy or when the choice is personal.
