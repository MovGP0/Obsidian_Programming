OkCupid uses political answers from its users to compare ideological diversity and age patterns among US political groups.

## Key findings

- The Democratic group was more internally diverse in the selected measures.
- Political alignment varied with age.
- The analysis links group composition to party cohesion, but the dating-site sample is not a voting-population sample.

## Algorithm relevance

This is a categorical clustering example. A broad label can contain several distinct preference groups, so a matcher should retain item-level answers.
