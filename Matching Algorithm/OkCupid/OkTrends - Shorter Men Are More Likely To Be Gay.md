This micro-post reports a correlation between men's reported height and their reported sexual orientation, with higher rates at the height extremes.

## Key findings

- The strongest reported difference was among very short men.
- The author states that the cause is unknown.
- The post asks for hypotheses instead of establishing a mechanism.

## Algorithm relevance

The item is a caution about proxy features. A measurable correlation can be predictive while still being sensitive, confounded, or unsuitable for ranking.
